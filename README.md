About:
Nyhil-Barren will be a Black-Metal inspired icon pack which will fork from Papirus in the beginning (check AUTHORS for more information),
for people that are tired of the conventional Coffee-Esque vibes or Vaporwave adjacent "rices".
Right now this is a solo project by yours truly- Silend or SilendSilv that will once i'll leave system icon purgatory include custom
icons for your beloved apps (E.g: Steam, Firefox, Ghostty).

Nyhil-Barren isnt made to look good, professional level or even appealing, the whole idea is for it to look abrasive, washed out and amateur-made.
Sadly- due to me not owning a good (or in this case bad) Camera or Phone Camera i cant just go into the woods and shoot weird unassuming pictures and add a monochrome filter to it,
however i try to keep the essence of the Amateur feeling by.. being a genuine Amateur, once again keep in mind that this is a passion project for me, if anyone wants to contribute
or add to it i would be glad as long as we keep something in mind: the spirit of Black Metal is to be ugly, abrasive, desolate and quite saddening.

Every file is upscaled/downscaled from a fixed resolution, this isnt because im genuinely that lazy or unknowledgeable, its like mentioned before- ment to be abrasive,
losing details as we scale down, and retaining blur/noise as we scale up, this is by design.

Includes: 
It is planned to be an Icon Pack, that means things like your Settings icon, or Sound icon will be changed, however no deep themes like how Folders look like inside Dolphin or similar.
This could be added in the future but dont count on my motivation cycle to be planning this in right now

Install: 
Currently no installation tutorial as its still a thought, although once the Project grows it will have a clear installation tutorial and how to set it up,

AI Notice:
The icons themselves are fully mouse-drawn in GIMP, there is currently and will never be any AI/Diffusion Model generating pictures for ANY of the icons.
I am not AI opposed but this is like i said- a passion project, to include AI into drawing the things i want to draw is just going against the nature of this repo
and also.. AI would make the icons look good, wich will make them look bad for what this is supposed to be.

This is a prototype README, things can and will change once the project grows.
