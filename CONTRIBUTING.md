The contribution system here is simple, if you want to be a contributor
give me a semi-formal Email <SilendSilv@protonmail.com> and I will look into it.

Rule of Thumb, if I don't answer with a no or yes after 10 days then expect it to be a no.. look into
latest commits, if I'm not committing.. then the project probably died, however if I am
and I don't answer my emails then expect me to just not bother to answer to you specifically
not because I have a grudge.. but because I found the EMAIL nonsensical enough to not give it
the time of day it wants.

After I've accepted you as a contributor, you will be given your own branch where you can freely play
around with, then if you want your branch to be included you will need to open a Pull Request into Main,
after I reviewed it, it will either be pulled into main and merged, or I will deny it with a written statement
as to why- my ignorance is reserved for people I don't know, not for the contributors I've accepted..
expect me to be precise about the "why" on everything to tedium if you're a part of the team.

Things to include in your EMAIL:
1. An Icon.. duh, best if it has a few more so I can see the thematic consistency and whether it 
fits into what's already there.

2. Your GitHub page, I won't be recruiting based on how many art commits you're having, but I will look
into your history and commits in other projects, >NEW ACCOUNTS MIGHT NOT GET ADDED TO CONTRIBUTOR'S<

3. A little statement why you want to join, at least 100 words or a few sentences should be enough
it's to see if your philosophy aligns with the one of the project, can't have "rainbow and sunshine" here.

These are the Standards here:
1. No AI, like mentioned in my "Manifesto", AI will make it look generic and clean
not our mission here guys

2. Semi-Artistic experience, while the Project might be lightweight from an Art perspective
it's only a red herring, there is a difference between "made to look bad" and "scribbled random things onto an icon"

3. Respectful Behaviour, this project is Amateur-like and unprofessional by design, however where I'm not unprofessional
is in the treatment of other humans, one insensitive joke won't get you banned but it will give you a warning
but things like grooming, harassment, discrimination against ANY race/ethnicity/religion will have you kicked
off the team and your assets ceased, we can't undo the commits, but we can undo the art.

4. Follow the Nyhil-Barren/ Folder to its core, all the sizes are fixed, .svg vector magic is way too clean, every picture has to have
a size for every size this icon-pack offers, no more, no less.

5. Use tools or self-made script or tools, how I do it is I take a segment of papirus-dark for example 22x22/status,
convert it via inkscape to a PNG 64x64 (I think it's a good middle ground between the finished sizes)
then I draw over the Picture of a Papirus-Icon (that's where the fork comes in)
and lastly I use a script to resize it down to the minimal size, and up to the maximum.. why? like I said in the README
the noise and loss of detail is BY DESIGN, this isn't your cozy Cappuccin rice.
